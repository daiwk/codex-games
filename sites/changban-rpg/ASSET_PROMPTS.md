# Tripo 素材提示词

山贼已接入游戏，下一批优先做枪兵、刀盾兵和剧情 NPC。保留山贼提示词供后续变体参考。以下每段可独立复制；每次只生成一个物件或一个人物，不要把整套场景塞进同一个 GLB。

人物导出建议：GLB，完整人形、标准 T-pose，脚底在同一平面，手指分开、双腿略分开；约 1–2 万三角面，PBR 贴图 1024–2048。先交无动画模型即可，绑定后再接游戏动作。角色和武器分别生成；盾牌也单独生成，人物空手。请保留可编辑原件，不在 Tripo 中合并人物、马和场景。

## 1. 山贼（已接入，可做变体）

```text
A full-body realistic game-ready Chinese roadside bandit from the late Han / Three Kingdoms era. Adult male, weathered face, dark brown cloth tunic, worn linen trousers, rope belt, simple leather wrist wraps and cloth shoes, small red headband. Modest layered clothing, readable silhouette, grounded historical fantasy style matching realistic Chinese warrior characters. Standard symmetrical T-pose, straight relaxed fingers, empty open hands, feet flat on one ground plane, two legs slightly separated. One single human character only, no weapon, no shield, no horse, no floor, no scenery. Clean animation-friendly topology, 10,000–20,000 triangles, PBR materials, 2K texture maximum, separate arms and legs with no clothing fused across joints, GLB export.
```
