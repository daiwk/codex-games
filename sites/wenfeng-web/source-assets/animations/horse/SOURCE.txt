Horse.glb is the unmodified downloaded FBX2glTF conversion.
Horse.fbx and Horse.blend were original author files; redundant local copies
were removed on 2026-10-20. Their download provenance and hashes remain in
metadata.json. Horse.glb contains all reference geometry, skin and motions
used by the current preparation script.
The initial low-poly runtime preview removed only duplicate animation aliases;
geometry, skin bind data and all 13 unique authored clips remain unchanged.
The original GLB contains 26 clips: 13 unique + 13 AnimalArmature| aliases.
See metadata.json for hashes, clip durations, dimensions and saddle calibration.

Style: low-poly brown horse with a skeletal rig, flat material colours and no textures.
The source is suitable as a replaceable lightweight first horse; it is not a
photoreal or armoured warhorse. Coat colours and simple tack are runtime variants.

2026-10-19 user-supplied Tripo replacement horses:
sites/wenfeng-web/source-assets/mounts/马_棕色.glb and 马_白色.glb are kept as original user assets.
Their exported bones/IBM/weights are malformed and there are no animation clips.
The runtime horse_brown.glb and horse_white.glb preserve their meshes, UVs,
triangles and coat textures (2048 maximum in runtime), fitting a rebuilt rig
and separate left/right fore/hind limb weights to the Quaternius anatomy.
Idle/Walk/Gallop motion is retargeted from this CC0 reference; the user
Tripo geometry/textures are NOT represented as CC0 licensed.
Run sites/wenfeng-web/tools/prepare_mounts.py to reproduce runtime assets.
mount_calibration.json records raw hoof/leg slices, axis and body centres.
Runtime manifest.json records source hashes, scale, bounds and saddle anchors.

Final retarget constraints: anatomical bone lengths remain fixed in all clips;
independent IK foot roots follow their matching fitted leg-chain ankle endpoint.
Whole tail-hair components are selected using welded-position connectivity;
this analysis never changes any runtime vertex, index or UV. The full tail
follows Back rigidly (with a whole-bundle 25-degree upward/backward gallop
rotation about Tail2), and both ear chains follow Head rigidly, to preserve
the replacement models' strand/ear shapes instead of transferring mismatched
small secondary motions. The four hooves control ground alignment; long
tail hair is allowed to brush the ground during a gallop.
